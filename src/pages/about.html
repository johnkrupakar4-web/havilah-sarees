About Us

Our Story

A family-led handloom saree business built on love for craft, honesty and warm personal service.

How it began

A Love for Handloom Sarees

Havilah Sarees grew out of a simple, genuine love for handloom sarees — an admiration for the patience, skill and beauty that goes into each piece. That love is the heart of everything we do.

What began as a personal appreciation for weaves became a small, family-led business. We are not a big retailer; we are a family that wants to share the sarees we believe in — honestly described, carefully chosen, and offered with warmth.

What we stand for

Quality, Craft & Personal Care

Quality First

We choose sarees for genuine weave, texture and finish — the kind of quality you can see and feel.

Honest Craft

We describe each saree truthfully — the fabric, the weave and the care it needs.

A Family Approach

Every enquiry is answered personally. You will talk to someone who actually knows the sarees.

Right Saree, Right Occasion

Weddings, festivals, office, or everyday — we help you find the saree that fits your moment.

How we help you

Finding Your Perfect Saree

Tell us the occasion, your taste in colours, and the feel you are after — soft silk, crisp cotton, festive zari or something in between. We will shortlist sarees from the collection and describe each one honestly across WhatsApp, chat, or in person.

Whether it is your first saree or your fiftieth, we treat every purchase like it is for our own family member — because for us, it is.
